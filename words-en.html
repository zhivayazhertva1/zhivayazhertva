whatever

i am often told by people that i am a unique person

i know that it is just a polite way of saying freak or weirdo

i often think popular trends for are tacky. i hate trends so much; i think they are boring and that they ruin the uniqueness of the characters i love. but the truth is, i just don't have the ability to understand them.

if i could, i wouldn't want to be unique...

i want to play with everyone else normally

i want to be able to enjoy things without overthinking.

instead of hiding away like an edgelord teenager trying too hard to look cool

if i were like that, i could make more people happy.

i don't want to be so-called unique anymore

even if i lose myself, i don't care, because this so-called self was never something important in the first place.

please leave me alone. im just a stranger
